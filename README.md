# Scaley

**The scale that ignores your water weight and tells you the truth.**

A personal weight-loss app that installs on your phone's home screen. No app store needed.

## What it does
- **Snap a meal.** The AI splits the plate into each food, estimates the grams, adds hidden oil and sauces, and works out calories and macros. Know the real weight? Type the grams and it recalculates.
- **Describe or search.** Type "2 eggs and toast", or pick from 130+ foods, hawker dishes included.
- **Daily target.** A calorie ring, protein/carbs/fat left, and a straight answer: on target, over, or under-eating.
- **Trend weight.** Smooths out water-weight swings so you see real progress against your plan.
- **Smart maintenance.** After ~2 weeks of logs it works out your real maintenance calories.
- **Coach Scaley.** An AI coach that reads your actual logs: "What should I eat next?", "Review my week".
- Works offline. Your data stays on your phone. Use **Me → Copy backup** now and then.

## Install on iPhone
1. Open **https://muadzangullia.github.io/scaley/** in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**.

## AI key (free)
Get a free Google Gemini key at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey and paste it in **Me → AI**. A Claude key (`sk-ant-…`) also works. The key is stored only on your phone.
